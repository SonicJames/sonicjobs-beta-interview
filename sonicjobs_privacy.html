Privacy Notice

Beta Candidate Research Programme

Last updated: June 2026  ·  Version 1.0  ·  Applies to: SonicJobs Beta Interview & Signup

1 Who we are

This privacy notice applies to the SonicJobs beta candidate research programme operated by SonicJobs. We are the data controller for the personal information collected through this interview and signup process.

If you have any questions about how we handle your data, contact us at privacy@sonicjobs.com.

2 What we collect and why

When you complete the beta interview and signup form, we collect the following:

  • Contact details — your name, email address, and optionally your phone number, so we can send you a beta invite and communicate about the programme
  • Interview responses — your answers to questions about your job search habits, platform preferences, attitudes towards AI tools, privacy comfort levels, and willingness to pay. These are used anonymously and in aggregate to shape product decisions
  • Brand name ratings — your star ratings and preferences across the candidate brand names we're evaluating, used solely for internal naming decisions
  • Automatically derived flags — a summary label generated from your responses (e.g. "WTP: high", "AI-enthusiast") used to triage beta cohort fit. These are not shared externally
We do not collect: payment information, government IDs, sensitive personal data (health, ethnicity, religion, etc.), or any data about your actual job applications or employment history.

3 Legal basis for processing

We process your data on the basis of consent — the checkbox you ticked before submitting your details. You can withdraw consent at any time (see Section 6).

Interview responses used for product research are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in understanding candidate needs, provided they are anonymised before any analysis.

4 How long we keep your data

🗓
Retention period: 6 months from the date of submission.
Your personal data (name, email, phone) and linked interview responses are automatically deleted from our systems 6 months after you submit this form. If you join the beta and become an active user, a separate privacy notice will apply to your account data.

After 6 months, any data used for product research will be fully anonymised — meaning it cannot be linked back to you in any way.

5 Who has access to your data

  • SonicJobs team members directly involved in the beta programme (product, engineering, and research leads)
  • Google — your data is stored in Google Sheets, subject to Google's data processing terms. Google does not use your data for advertising or share it with third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties for marketing purposes. We do not use your data for automated decision-making that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

6 Your rights

Under GDPR and applicable privacy law, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data at any time before the 6-month automatic deletion
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Withdraw consent — opt out of the beta programme at any time with no consequence
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interest

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@sonicjobs.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7 Security

Your data is stored in a private Google Sheet accessible only to authorised SonicJobs team members. Submissions are protected by a server-side authentication token — publicly discoverable URLs cannot read or extract your data. We use HTTPS for all data in transit.

No system is completely secure. In the event of a data breach that affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours as required by GDPR.

8 Cookies

This beta interview page does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics scripts. We do not track your behaviour across other websites.

9 Contact & complaints

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights: privacy@sonicjobs.com
If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority. In the UK this is the ICO. In the EU, contact your local supervisory authority.
